## 1. Business Strategy & Positioning

### 1.1 Business Positioning
Celestial Contractor & Engineer Pvt. Ltd. (CCEPL) is a premier engineering, procurement, and civil construction contracting company specializing in commercial buildings, industrial infrastructure, turnkey EPC projects, and heavy civil utility developments.

### 1.2 Target Audience
- Government Infrastructure Procurement Officers & Municipal Bodies
- Corporate Enterprises, Industrial Plant Developers & Factory Owners
- Real Estate Developers & Commercial Complex Investors
- Project Management Consultants (PMCs) & Structural Architects

### 1.3 Key Conversion Goals & CTAs
- **Primary CTA**: *"Request an Engineering Quote"* / *"Consult Our EPC Team"*
- **Secondary CTA**: *"View Project Portfolio"* / *"Download Corporate Profile"*
- **Primary Goal**: Generate high-intent RFP (Request for Proposal) inquiries and corporate contract leads.

## 3. Brand System & Global Tokens

### 3.1 Color Palette
- **Primary Accent**: `#1E3A8A` (Deep Engineering Blue)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#F59E0B` (Industrial Construction Gold)
- **Supporting Cyan**: `#0284C7` (Structural Precision Cyan)
- **Dark Background/Text**: `#0F172A` (Midnight Slate)
- **Body Text Color**: `#334155` (Slate Gray)
- **Surface / Card Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) & `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-White)
- **Border / Divider**: `#E2E8F0` (Light Slate Border)

### 3.2 Global Typography
- **Heading Font Family**: `Montserrat`, sans-serif (Bold, Authoritative, Structural)
- **Body Font Family**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Highly Legible, Modern, Professional)
- **H1 (Hero Heading)**: 48px Desktop / 36px Tablet / 28px Mobile (Line Height: 1.2, Weight: 800)
- **H2 (Section Heading)**: 36px Desktop / 28px Tablet / 24px Mobile (Line Height: 1.25, Weight: 700)
- **H3 (Card / Sub-Heading)**: 24px Desktop / 20px Tablet / 18px Mobile (Line Height: 1.3, Weight: 600)
- **Body Text**: 16px Desktop / 15px Mobile (Line Height: 1.6, Weight: 400)

### 3.3 Container & Design Tokens
- **Container Max-Width**: `1240px` (Boxed Layout)
- **Section Padding**: `80px 20px` (Desktop) / `50px 15px` (Mobile)
- **Card Border Radius**: `12px`
- **Button Border Radius**: `6px`
- **Card Shadow**: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
