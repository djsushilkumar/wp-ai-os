## 2. Managing & Editing Pages in Elementor

1. Log in to WordPress Admin.
2. Navigate to **Pages** -> **All Pages**.
3. Hover over the page you want to modify (e.g. **Home** or **Services**) and click **Edit with Elementor**.
4. Use Elementor's visual drag-and-drop panel to update text, images, or layout styling.
5. Click the green **Update** button at the bottom left to save changes live.
